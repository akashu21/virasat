01 / WORLD IDENTITY

A world beyond the familiar

No-Land is a supernatural world into which people from Earth are summoned. Its society revolves around dungeons, their creators, the people who inhabit them and those who enter them.

Dungeons are constructed experiences containing rules, anomalies, challenges and rewards. Their creators develop them, while players and other inhabitants interact with them.

03 / CENTRAL SYSTEM

The Dungeon System

Creators construct dungeon spaces containing anomalies, rules, restrictions, challenges, objectives, rewards and escape conditions.

ESTABLISHED RULES

A creator cannot die inside their own dungeon. Dungeon design also needs meaningful possibilities and escape routes rather than completely unreasonable rules.

Dungeon creation and exploration connect to a wider economy involving creator points, rankings, gifts, professions, trade streets, houses, group chats, weekly runs and penalties. Exact formulas remain undocumented.
